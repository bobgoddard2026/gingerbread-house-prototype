Every morning starts with a warm hello.

Our doors open at 7:00. Your child is welcomed by name and helped to settle in before you head to work.

Morning ✎ 11

Learning through play.

Circle time and stories, then learning centers for building, art, and make-believe. Our classrooms are small, so every child gets a teacher's attention.

Midday ✎ 12

Lunch together, then a rest.

After lunch the lights go down for nap and quiet time. Our infants keep their own schedules for feeding and sleep.

Afternoon ✎ 13

Afternoons full of surprises.

Outdoor play, songs, and games. Once a month, Wildlife Encounters brings animals for the children to meet up close, and holidays bring special visitors and parties.

What ages do you care for?

We care for children from six weeks to six years old. ✎ 4

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. We are closed on weekends. ✎ 9

Do you have openings right now?

Openings depend on your child's age. Please call (603) 886-1500 and we will tell you what is open. ✎ 7

How much does it cost?

Please call us for current rates. ✎ 24

Do you accept the NH Child Care Scholarship?

Yes. If your family gets help paying for child care through the state's NH Child Care Scholarship, you can use it here. ✎ 23

Are you licensed?

Yes. The State of New Hampshire licenses us as a group child care program for up to 60 children. Our license number is CCCB-06568. Our record, including the state's inspection visits, is public on the state's Child Care Search website. ✎ 22

How do I find you?

We are in Lobster Boat Plaza, the one-story brick plaza at 273 Derry Road (Route 102) in Litchfield, next to the Lobster Boat Restaurant. Look for our name on the plaza's sign at the road. ✎ 32

Are you the Gingerbread House that used to be in Hudson?

Yes. We are the same family business, now in Litchfield. Some old websites still show our Hudson addresses, and a few even say closed. We are open. ✎ 33
